How it works

From your first deck to exam day.

  1. 1

    Pick a deck

    Choose a ready deck for Semester, Placement or Competitive exams, or make your own.

  2. 2

    Say how sure you are

    Tap Confident or Not sure before you see the answer. The app keeps track of both.

  3. 3

    Fix weak spots

    Weak topics are found for you, with an explanation and practice cards.

  4. 4

    Follow the plan

    Each day you get a short plan. Exam decks and weak topics come first.
