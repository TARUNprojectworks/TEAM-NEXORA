What you can do

  • {{ icon("note") }}Study

    Smart Study picks the most useful card each time, using five shelves and how confident you felt. Quick Revise lets you go through a whole deck, shuffled.

  • {{ icon("highlight") }}Fix weak spots

    The app finds topics you keep missing and cards you were sure about but got wrong, explains them and adds practice cards.

  • {{ icon("calendar") }}Plan and track

    A short plan for today on Home, Exam mode for decks with a date, and mastery, streak and progress per deck on My Progress.

How it works

  1. Pick a deck. Add one from the Deck Library (Semester, Placement, Competitive) or make your own by typing cards, pasting notes or uploading a photo.
  2. Say how sure you are. Before you flip a card, tap Confident or Not sure. Then say how it went.
  3. Fix weak spots. Weak topics are found for you, with an explanation and practice cards.
  4. Follow today's plan. Home shows what to study today, with exam decks and weak topics first.
